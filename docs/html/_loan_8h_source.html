15/*
16Formulas from: http://oakroadsystems.com/math/loan.htm
17
18Loan balance after n payments have been made:
19 B_n = A*(1+i)^n - (P/i)*((1+i)^n - 1)
20
21
22Payment amount on a loan:
23 P = i*A / (1 - (1+i)^-N)
24
25
26Number of payments on a loan: (You can use any logarithm base, as long as both logs use the same base.)
27 Aunt Sally offers to lend you $3500 at 6% for that new home theater system you want.
28 If you pay her back $100 a month, how long will it take?
29 Solution: 6% per year is 0.5% per month, or 0.005. P = 100 and A = 3500. N = 38.57
30 N = -log(1-i*A/P) / log(1+i)
31
32
33Original loan amount:
34 A = (P/i)*(1 - (1+i)^-N)
35
36
37Interest Rate:
38 i = (((1 + P/A)^(1/q) - 1 )^q - 1) NOTICE: This is an approximate not an exact solution
39 where q = log(1+1/N) / log(2)
40
41
42Variables:
43A the loan amount (the principal sum) or initial investment
44B_n or Bn (pronounced B sub n) the balance after n payments have been made. After the last payment has been made, B_N is zero.)
45i the interest rate per period, not per year
46 (For instance, if the loan payments are made monthly and the interest rate is 9%, then i = 9%/12 = 0.75% = 0.0075.)
47n the number of time periods elapsed at any given point
48N the total number of payments for the entire loan or investment
49P the amount of each equal payment
50*/
